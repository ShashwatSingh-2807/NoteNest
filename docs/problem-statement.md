# Problem Statement

## Background
College learning resources such as lecture notes, lab manuals, previous-year question papers, and reference documents are often shared through messaging groups, personal drives, and direct exchanges.

## Problem
Resources are fragmented across channels. Students may struggle to find the correct material, identify its subject or semester, or locate a useful version. Informal sharing offers limited organization and moderation.

## Proposed Solution
NoteNest will provide a centralized academic resource-sharing platform where students can upload, organize, search, access, and download resources using academic categories.

## Intended Users
- **Students:** discover and contribute academic resources.
- **Administrators:** manage academic categories and moderate shared resources.

## Expected Outcome
A usable platform with structured resource discovery, controlled access, and a collaborative development workflow. See [PRD.md](PRD.md) for the initial product scope.
